## 🛡️ Security Domains - Quick Links

### Black Hat (Threat Intelligence)
- **Focus**: Understand attacker techniques defensively
- **Key Tools**: MITRE ATT&CK, Metasploitable, Bug Bounty programs
- **Cost**: $0-500
- **Timeline**: 1-2 years to proficiency
- **Why Hard**: Asymmetric warfare, adaptive evasion

### Red Team (Offensive Security)
- **Focus**: Ethical penetration testing, full kill chain
- **Key Certs**: OSCP ($1,500), SANS FOR610 ($7,000)
- **Key Tools**: HackTheBox, Cobalt Strike, BloodHound
- **Cost**: $2,000-10,000
- **Timeline**: 1-3 years to mastery

### Blue Team (Defensive Security)
- **Focus**: Detection, monitoring, incident response
- **Key Certs**: BTL1 ($400), GCED ($2,000), CISSP ($700)
- **Key Tools**: ELK Stack, Suricata, Wazuh, Splunk
- **Cost**: $500-3,000
- **Timeline**: 1-3 years to mastery

### Purple Team (Red+Blue Integration)
- **Focus**: Collaborative security testing, detection engineering
- **Key Certs**: CREST CRT ($3,000)
- **Key Tools**: Caldera, Atomic Red Team, VECTR
- **Cost**: $1,000-5,000
- **Prerequisites**: 1-2 years red OR blue experience

### Forensics
- **Focus**: Digital evidence analysis, incident investigation
- **Key Certs**: CHFI ($1,200), SANS FOR500 ($7,000)
- **Key Tools**: Autopsy, Volatility, FTK Imager
- **Cost**: $1,000-8,000
- **Timeline**: 1-3 years to mastery

### CIA-Level Ops (Intelligence)
- **Focus**: OSINT, intelligence analysis (legal/hypothetical only)
- **Key Certs**: CISSP-ISSAP ($700), CSI Linux ($500)
- **Key Tools**: Maltego, Shodan, OSINT Framework
- **Cost**: $500-2,000
- **Practice**: TraceLabs CTFs (helping find missing persons)

- **Key Resources**: Qiskit (Free), Nielsen & Chuang textbook ($70)
- **Cost**: $0-500
- **Why Hardest**: Non-intuitive physics, heavy math
- **Timeline**: 2-3 years to competency

### AI/ML
- **Focus**: Neural networks, transformers, LLMs
- **Key Resources**: Fast.ai (Free), Andrew Ng Coursera (Free)
- **Key Tools**: PyTorch, TensorFlow, Hugging Face, Ollama
- **Cost**: $0-1,000
- **Timeline**: 1-3 years to production-ready

### Biotechnology

## 🔧 Lab Setup - Minimums

### Security Lab
- **CPU**: 4+ cores
- **RAM**: 16GB
- **Storage**: 500GB SSD
- **VMs**: Kali, Ubuntu, Metasploitable, Windows
- **Cost**: ~$800-1,500 for hardware (or use existing)

### Engineering Lab
- **CPU**: High core count
- **RAM**: 32GB
- **Storage**: 1TB SSD
- **GPU**: Optional NVIDIA for AI/ML
- **Cost**: ~$1,500-3,000 (GPU adds $500-1,000)

### Cloud Alternative
- **Google Colab**: Free GPU for ML
- **AWS Free Tier**: Limited free resources
- **HackTheBox**: $20/month pentesting labs
- **TryHackMe**: $10/month beginner-friendly
