**Core Equation:**
```

Metrics:
- sin(θ) = vertical waveform component
- cos(θ) = horizontal waveform component  
- tan(θ) = slope / rate of change
- Resonance = 1 - |drift|
- Drift = (θ_current - θ_baseline) / θ_baseline
```

6. Iterate until fitness threshold met
7. Auto-commit successful genomes to repository
