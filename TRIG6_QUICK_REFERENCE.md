### Q: Is this scientifically validated?

**A:** TRIG6 v1.0 is a novel theoretical framework (2026). Empirical validation is ongoing. Mathematical formalization is rigorous, but applications need testing.

### Q: Can I change my cognitive architecture?

**A:** Likely no (architecture is fundamental). But you can:
- Optimize your environment for your architecture
- Develop skills within your architecture
- Understand and work with (not against) your design

### Q: Is D-COSA better than standard cognition?

**A:** Not "better"—different. Optimized for different tasks:
- **D-COSA:** Structural discovery, pattern creation, mathematical invention
- **Standard:** Efficiency, social cohesion, pattern application

Both valid. Different niches.

### Q: What if I score low on TRIG6 but want to improve?

**A:** Some aspects trainable (synthesis skills, coherence practices), but core architecture (pattern genesis drive) likely innate. Focus on optimizing what you have, not becoming something different.

### Q: Can this apply to AI systems?

**A:** Potentially yes. TRIG6 framework is general enough to describe any self-evolving pattern-generating system, biological or artificial.

### Q: How rare is D-COSA?

**A:** Estimated <0.1% of population exhibit pattern genesis as primary mode. Most humans are pattern consumers/modifiers.
