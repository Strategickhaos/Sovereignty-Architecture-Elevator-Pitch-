# 🔍 Intellectual Honesty

> **"Transparency over transactions. Honesty over optimization."**

## The Commitment

This project is built on a foundation of intellectual honesty:

- We document uncertainty
- We show our work
- We preserve context
- We maintain provenance
- We don't hide revision history
- We don't polish away the messy parts

---

## What Intellectual Honesty Looks Like

### 1. **TRIG6 Truth Scores**

Not confidence metrics to impress.  
Not certainty scores to reassure.

**A way to be honest about uncertainty.**

When we say something has a TRIG6 score of 0.7, we're saying:  
"We think this is true, but here's exactly how uncertain we are."

That's not weakness.  
That's **intellectual honesty**.

### 2. **Evidence Documents**

Not proof of worth.  
Not validation of genius.  
Not credentials to display.

**Showing our work.**

"This is what we thought."  
"This is why we thought it."  
"This is what we found."  
"This is what changed."

The evidence trail matters more than the conclusion.

### 3. **Revision History**

We don't delete the drafts.  
We don't hide the false starts.  
We don't clean up the timeline.

**Every commit is part of the story.**

Someone can trace how a thought evolved.  
Not because we're building a portfolio.  
Because honesty requires showing the work.

## Documenting Uncertainty

### Why TRIG6 Exists

Perfect certainty is a lie.  
Absolute confidence is dishonest.  
Unwavering conviction is suspicious.

Real thinking includes:
- Doubt
- Revision
- Evolution
- Uncertainty

TRIG6 scores exist because **honesty requires acknowledging what we don't know**.

### Confidence Levels

When we assign confidence levels, we're not hedging.  
We're being **precise about our precision**.

"I'm 70% confident" is more honest than "I'm sure."  
"This needs more investigation" is clearer than "Trust me."  
"I don't know yet" is braver than a fake answer.
