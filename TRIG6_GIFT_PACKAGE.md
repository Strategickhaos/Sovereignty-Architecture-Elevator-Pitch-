### Statement
In a TRIG6-gated evolutionary system:
```
F_{n+1} ≥ F_n · (1 - D_avg)
```

Where:
- F_n = mean population fitness at generation n
- D_avg = average drift penalty (bounded by TRIG6 pruning)

### Proof Sketch
1. **Setup:** Population P_n = {x_i}, fitness f(x) = R(1-D)(1-N)eq

5. **Condition:** R·Δf ≥ D·F (resource boost exceeds drift loss)
6. **QED:** Monotonic improvement guaranteed ∎
