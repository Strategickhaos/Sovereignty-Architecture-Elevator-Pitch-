## IV. Legal Framework for IP Ownership

### Enterprise AI Account Terms
Enterprise AI service agreements include provisions such as:

> "Customer owns all input data, output data, and any work product created using the Services. Customer retains all intellectual property rights in Customer Data."

This creates a legal chain of custody:
1. **Organization** → owns enterprise AI accounts
2. **Enterprise Accounts** → produce outputs owned by customer
3. **Customer (Organization)** → owns all outputs
4. **Organization Members** → authorized to use outputs

### Cannot Plagiarize Own IP
**Legal Principle**: Plagiarism requires unauthorized use of someone else's work.

Since the Organization:
- Owns the AI accounts
- Owns the proprietary input systems
- Owns the AI outputs per service agreements
- Licenses outputs to authorized users

...it is legally impossible for the Organization or its authorized members to plagiarize AI-generated content derived from proprietary systems.
