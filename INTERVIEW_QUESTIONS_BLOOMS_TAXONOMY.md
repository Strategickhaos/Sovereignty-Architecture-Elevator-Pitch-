## Overview

These questions are designed for Computer Science (CS) and Behavioral Science (BS) roles, focusing on the "Creating" level of Bloom's Taxonomy. They require candidates to design, invent, or synthesize new systems, applications, or frameworks utilizing **FlameLang/SAGCO** (a sovereign AI-governed compute organism). Questions are categorized by field (10-15 per category for balance), emphasizing innovative utilization across domains.

### Bloom's Taxonomy - Creating Level

At the Creating level (the highest tier), candidates are expected to:
- **Design** novel systems and architectures
- **Invent** new approaches and methodologies
- **Synthesize** multiple concepts into coherent solutions
- **Create** original frameworks and implementations

### About FlameLang/SAGCO

- **FlameLang**: A sovereign symbolic language system with glyph-based execution models
- **SAGCO**: Sovereign AI-Governed Compute Organism
- **Key Concepts**: Quantum-entangled layers, PID-RANCO control, multi-AI ratification, physics-enforced types, codon mappings, behavioral DNA profiling
