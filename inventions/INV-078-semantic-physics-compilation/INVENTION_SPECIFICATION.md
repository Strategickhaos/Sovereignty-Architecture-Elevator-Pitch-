## FIELD OF THE INVENTION

This invention relates to computational physics, quantum gravity phenomenology, and symbolic computation. Specifically, it concerns a novel method for translating human-readable physical descriptions into executable mathematical models through a semantically-grounded compilation pipeline.

## BACKGROUND

### The Problem

Current approaches to theoretical physics suffer from several limitations:

1. **Language Barrier**: Mathematical formalism requires years of training, creating a bottleneck between physical intuition and executable models.

2. **Framework Lock-in**: Researchers trained in specific approaches (String Theory, Loop Quantum Gravity, etc.) become constrained by the assumptions of their framework.

3. **Discrete-Continuous Gap**: No existing system provides a unified representation that spans discrete symbolic reasoning and continuous mathematical functions.

4. **Theory Compilation**: No tools exist to automatically compile natural language physical descriptions into runnable simulations.

### Prior Approaches

- **Symbolic Math Systems** (Mathematica, Maple): Operate on mathematical expressions but lack semantic grounding in physical meaning
- **Physics Simulation Frameworks** (COMSOL, ANSYS): Require pre-defined equations, do not generate models from intent
- **Natural Language Processing**: Can parse text but cannot generate executable physics models
- **Domain-Specific Languages**: Exist for specific physics domains but lack universal semantic primitives

**NONE** of these approaches combine semantic grounding, discrete-continuous transformation, and physical constraint enforcement in a unified compilation pipeline.
