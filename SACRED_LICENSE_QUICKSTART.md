1. **First Violation**: Public notice + mandatory remediation plan
2. **Second Violation**: Temporary suspension of deployment license
3. **Third Violation or Severe Harm**: Permanent revocation of all rights
4. **Willful Malice**: Legal action + public documentation

The Sacred License respects good faith efforts:
- **Mistakes happen** → Acknowledge, fix, learn

- **Intent counts** → Good intentions + corrective action = second chances
- **Malice is different** → Intentional harm = immediate consequences
