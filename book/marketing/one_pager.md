# THE SISTER PROTOCOL — One-Page Overview

## The Book in 60 Seconds

**Title:** *THE SISTER PROTOCOL: How a Brother, a Legion of AIs, and a New Math Took Aim at "Incurable"*

**Genre:** Narrative Nonfiction / Memoir / Technology

**Length:** 70,000-80,000 words

**The Hook:**  
A rope access pipefitter who couldn't exit vim builds enterprise-grade AI infrastructure, invents new mathematics, and creates a sovereign operating system—all to keep a promise to his sister with an incurable neurological disease.

## The Story in Three Acts

### Act I: The Promise
Dom's sister—born with one arm, later diagnosed with an incurable condition—challenges his drunk promise to build AI that could help her: "You can't even program." He searches "hardest command line to learn," finds vim, gets trapped for 20 minutes. That humiliation becomes fuel.

### Act II: The Build
Over 18 months, while working full-time as a pipefitter earning $160K+, Dom:
- Teaches himself to code from scratch (no CS degree yet)
- Builds a 4-node Kubernetes cluster (Athena, Lyra, Nova, iPower)
- Invents TRIG6 (mathematical framework for AI health monitoring)
- Creates FlameLang (programming language compiling through DNA primitives)
- Designs SAGCO-OS (sovereign operating system versioned as genetic strands)
- Files legal entities with 7% irrevocable charitable allocation to medical research

### Act III: The Mission
Dom maps 36 neurological diseases to TRIG6 parameters, coordinates a "Legion of Minds" (Claude, GPT, Grok, local models) through consensus protocols, and builds infrastructure designed to contribute to medical research breakthroughs. The question: Did it help?
