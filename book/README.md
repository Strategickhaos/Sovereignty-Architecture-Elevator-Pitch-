# The Sister Protocol

**How a Brother, a Legion of AIs, and a New Math Took Aim at "Incurable"**

---

## About This Book

This is the manuscript for "The Sister Protocol" - a non-fiction work that sits at the intersection of memoir, engineering case study, and manifesto for sovereign AI and medical research.

**Genre:** Non-fiction – memoir of learning + obsession, engineering case study, manifesto for sovereign AI and medical research.

**Think:** *The Phoenix Project* × *The Emperor of All Maladies* × *A Mind for Numbers*, but written from inside a multi-monitor cockpit.

---

## Book Structure

The manuscript is organized into four parts:

### Part I – The Wound
Chapters 1-3: The beginning – a sister's diagnosis, a drunken promise, and learning to program the hard way.

### Part II – Building a Legion of Minds  
Chapters 4-8: The technical journey – creating TRIG6, FlameLang, SAGCO-OS, and assembling a multi-AI consensus system.

### Part III – The Sister Protocol
Chapters 9-12: The mission – hardcoding compassion, mapping diseases to waveforms, and attempting to simulate a cure.

### Part IV – Sovereignty and the Future
Chapters 13-16: The implications – sovereign AI, cognitive architecture, and what happens if/when the system works.
