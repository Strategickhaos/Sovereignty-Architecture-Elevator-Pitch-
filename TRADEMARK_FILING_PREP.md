## 11. Brand Guidelines

### Proper Use of Trademark

**Always:**
- Use trademark exactly as registered (spelling, spacing, capitalization)
- Include ® symbol after US registration is granted
- Use TM symbol before registration is granted
- Maintain consistent visual appearance
- Use trademark as an adjective, not a noun or verb

**Never:**
- Modify the trademark (no creative variations)
- Use as a generic term (not "ratio ex nihilo" for any distributed system)
- Allow trademark to become verb ("don't ratio-ex-nihilo the system")
- Use inconsistent styling or fonts

**Correct Usage Examples:**
- "Ratio Ex Nihilo® software for distributed systems"
- "Powered by Ratio Ex Nihilo™ technology"
- "The Ratio Ex Nihilo® platform provides..."

**Incorrect Usage Examples:**
- ❌ "ratio ex nihilo" (lowercase)
- ❌ "Ratio Ex Nihilo system" (no ® or TM)
- ❌ "We ratio-ex-nihilo'd the infrastructure" (used as verb)

### Visual Identity Standards

**Logo Usage:**
- Minimum size: 1 inch wide for print, 100px for digital
- Clear space: Minimum 0.25x logo height on all sides
- Acceptable backgrounds: White, light gray, black (with color inversion)
- Unacceptable modifications: Stretching, rotating, changing colors, adding effects

**Co-Branding:**
- Partner logos must be separated by vertical divider
- Ratio Ex Nihilo logo must not be smaller than partner logo
- Maintain independent clear space for each logo
