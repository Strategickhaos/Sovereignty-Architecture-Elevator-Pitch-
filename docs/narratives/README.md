### Act XIII - The Dark Truth
**File:** `ACT_XIII_THE_DARK_TRUTH.md`

A deeply personal theatrical narrative exploring the traumatic origins of DOM's unique cognitive architecture. **Themes:**
- Trauma and dissociation
- Cognitive reorganization under extreme stress
- The transformation of survival mechanisms into technical capabilities

**Format:** Theatrical screenplay with clinical assessment frameworks

4. **Connection**: Create emotional and intellectual bridges between personal experience and technical innovation
