# THE DOM OPERATING SYSTEM
## A Seven-Act Play About Sovereignty, Security, and the Sister Protocol

---

### Overview

**THE DOM OPERATING SYSTEM** is a theatrical exploration of how one engineer's quest to build secure, sovereign infrastructure for medical research becomes a movement. What begins as a technical challenge—building unhackable systems—transforms into a philosophy of perpetual motion, self-funding infrastructure, and collaborative swarm intelligence.

At its heart lies a mission: **To build the cure. For the sister. For all families waiting.**
