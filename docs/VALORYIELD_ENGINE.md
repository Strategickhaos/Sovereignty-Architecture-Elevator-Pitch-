## Executive Summary

ValorYield Engine is a Wyoming nonprofit corporation operating as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt charitable organization. The entity receives **7% of net revenues** from Strategickhaos DAO LLC via the automated SwarmGate Protocol, creating a sustainable funding mechanism for charitable purposes.

The mission encompasses AI-driven dividend allocation to underserved communities, supporting basic needs (vehicle/housing), credit defense tools, and financial literacy programs.

## 3. Funding Mechanism

### 3.1 7% Allocation from Strategickhaos DAO LLC

**Legal Obligation:**
Strategickhaos DAO LLC is contractually bound to allocate **7% of net revenues** to ValorYield Engine as first priority before any other distributions.

**Reference:** [Articles of Organization DAO LLC](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1VU7zxiL9EtOpN2_mVVtIx69Ijsu4agDQWQk_DJuyUqg/edit)

**Calculation Method:**
```
Gross Revenue = All income from operations
Operating Costs = Legitimate business expenses
Net Revenue = Gross Revenue - Operating Costs
ValorYield Allocation = Net Revenue × 0.07
```

### 3.2 Transfer Mechanism

**System:** SwarmGate Protocol v1.0  
**Automation:** Fully automated with manual oversight  
**Frequency:** Monthly reconciliation, quarterly distribution  
**Method:** ACH, Wire Transfer, or Cryptocurrency  
**Verification:** Cryptographic timestamping + dual signature

**Reference:** [SWARMGATE_PROTOCOL.md](./SWARMGATE_PROTOCOL.md)

### 3.3 Distribution Timeline

| Period | Activity |
|--------|----------|
| **Monthly** | Revenue reconciliation, allocation calculation |
| **Quarterly** | Bulk transfer to ValorYield Engine |
| **Annually** | IRS Form 990 filing, impact reporting |
