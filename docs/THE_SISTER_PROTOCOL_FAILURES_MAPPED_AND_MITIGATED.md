## Technical Notes

### TRIG6 Parameter Definitions

**θ (Phase Angle):** Represents the progression stage of a failure mode
- π/4 (45°): Early-stage risk, emerging threat
- π/2 (90°): Mid-stage risk, active concern
- π (180°): Late-stage risk, critical condition
- 3π/2 (270°): Catastrophic risk, system-threatening

**R (Resonance):** System stability and coherence (0-1 scale)
- High R (>0.5): Stable, predictable behavior
- Low R (<0.5): Unstable, at-risk condition

**D (Deviation):** Variance from expected behavior (0-1 scale)
- High D (>0.5): Significant drift from baseline
- Low D (<0.5): Controlled, bounded variation

**N (Uncertainty):** Measurement noise and prediction confidence (0-1 scale)
- High N (>0.5): Low confidence, high unpredictability
- Low N (<0.5): High confidence, measurable patterns

**Danger Criterion:** |tan θ| > 10
- Identifies failure modes in critical phase angles requiring immediate intervention

### Darwinian Evolution Mechanics

Mitigations evolve through competitive fitness gates where:
- f = fitness score of proposed mitigation
- champion = current best mitigation
- Selection occurs when f > champion
- Incremental improvements of +0.02 or greater trigger evolution
- Convergence target: eq (equilibrium) ≥ 0.99 for stable solutions
