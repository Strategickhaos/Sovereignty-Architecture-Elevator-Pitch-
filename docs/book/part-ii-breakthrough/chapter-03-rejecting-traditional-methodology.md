## The Einstein Parallel

**Albert Einstein, 1905: The Miracle Year**

- **Age:** 26
- **Position:** Patent clerk (third class)
- **Academic credentials:** None (failed to get a teaching position)
- **Institutional support:** Zero
- **Papers published that year:** 
  - Special relativity
  - Photoelectric effect (later won Nobel Prize)
  - Brownian motion
  - E = mc²

**What the establishment said:**  
*"He's a nobody."*

**What history said:**  
*"He rewrote physics."*

## The Ramanujan Parallel

**Srinivasa Ramanujan, 1913**

- **Education:** Failed out of college (twice)
- **Formal training in mathematics:** Minimal
- **Method:** Filled notebooks with theorems that came from intuition
- **Academic validation:** Sent letters to mathematicians who rejected him
- **What changed:** One person (G.H. Hardy) recognized genius
- **Result:** Discoveries mathematicians are still exploring 100+ years later

**What the establishment said:**  
*"These formulas make no sense."*

**What mathematics said:**  
*"These formulas are true, and we don't know why yet."*
