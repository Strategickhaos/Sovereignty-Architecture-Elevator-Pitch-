## ValorYield Engine PBC

### Entity Details

**Type:** Public Benefit Corporation  
**Jurisdiction:** Wyoming, United States  
**Status:** Active and in Good Standing  
**Formation Date:** [Per Wyoming Secretary of State filings]  
**Public Benefit:** Research, education, and cure development

### Purpose

ValorYield Engine PBC embodies the financial architecture that ensures:
1. **7% Irrevocable Commitment** to public benefit
2. **Mission permanence** through legal structure
3. **Profit + Purpose** alignment
4. **Sister Protocol** financial backing

### Public Benefit Corporation Status

**What PBC Means (Wyoming Statute):**

Under Wyoming law, a Public Benefit Corporation:
- Must identify public benefit in charter
- Directors must consider public benefit in decisions
- Cannot subordinate public benefit to shareholder returns
- Must file annual benefit reports
- Mission is legally protected

**Our Charter Provisions:**

1. **7% Equity Lock:**
   - 7% of all equity permanently dedicated to public benefit
   - Irrevocable (cannot be changed by future boards)
   - Compounds over time
   - Automatically allocated to research

2. **Public Benefit Definition:**
   - Medical research (NEURO-36 genome development)
   - Cure engines and treatment optimization
   - Accessibility and cognitive tools
   - Open-source AI development

3. **Governance Integration:**
   - Sister Protocol framework
   - TRIG6-based decision systems
   - Legion of Minds consensus
   - Transparent allocation

### Why This Structure Matters

**Traditional For-Profit:**  
- Fiduciary duty = maximize shareholder value
- Mission can be sacrificed for profits
- Commitments can be reversed

**Traditional Nonprofit:**  
- Dependent on donations
- No equity value to compound
- Can run out of funding

**Public Benefit Corporation:**  
- Legally required to serve mission
- Can generate profits to sustain mission
- Equity commitment compounds forever
- Best of both worlds

### The 7% Mechanism

**How it works:**

```
Company Equity Value: $X
7% Dedicated to Public Benefit: $0.07X
Remaining for Growth/Returns: $0.93X

As company grows:
$1M → $70K to research
$10M → $700K to research
$100M → $7M to research
$1B → $70M to research

The 7% grows WITH the company, not FROM profits.
```

**Why 7% specifically:**

- Large enough to matter (compound to billions over time)
- Small enough to sustain growth (93% remains)
- Biblically significant (covenant, completion, rest)
- Economically optimal (maximizes impact without compromising viability)

## Legal Timeline & Verification

### Why Timing Matters

**Traditional invention:** Build → Patent → Legal structure  
**Our approach:** Legal structure → Document claims → Build → Prove

**Why reverse order?**

1. **Protection first:** IP rights established before public disclosure
2. **Credibility:** Legal entities prove seriousness
3. **Timestamps:** Corporate records document invention timeline
4. **Sovereignty:** Own the legal framework before needing it

## Legal Lessons Learned

### 1. **Structure Before Need**

Don't wait until you need legal protection to create it.

By the time you're in a dispute, it's too late.

### 2. **Document Everything**

Timestamps matter.  
Witnesses matter.  
Notarization matters.

**Future proof: "I invented this on [date]" needs evidence.**

### 3. **Irrevocability Is Powerful**

If something can be undone, assume it will be.

**Build permanence from the beginning.**

### 4. **Integrate Legal + Technical**

Legal structures protect IP.  
Technical structures (SAGCO-OS, TRIG6) enable sovereignty.

**Together, they create unstoppable systems.**
