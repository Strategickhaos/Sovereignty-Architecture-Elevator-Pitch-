# CMB Polarization Data Analysis: ΛCDM vs Loop Quantum Cosmology

## Overview

This analysis compares the standard ΛCDM (Lambda Cold Dark Matter) cosmological model with Loop Quantum Cosmology (LQC) using Planck 2018 CMB (Cosmic Microwave Background) polarization data.

## Background

### CMB Polarization Modes

The CMB polarization can be decomposed into two modes:

1. **E-mode (EE)**: Electric-type polarization pattern, sourced by scalar (density) perturbations
2. **B-mode (BB)**: Magnetic-type polarization pattern, sourced by tensor (gravitational wave) perturbations and gravitational lensing
3. **TE Cross-correlation**: Temperature-E-mode cross-correlation, probes velocity-temperature correlations

### ΛCDM Model

The standard cosmological model includes:
- Cold Dark Matter (CDM)
- Dark Energy (Λ - cosmological constant)  
- Baryonic matter
- Photons and neutrinos

The model provides excellent fits to CMB data but has some tensions:
- Large-scale TT power suppression (~2-3σ anomaly)
- Lensing amplitude discrepancy (A_lens > 1 in TT vs A_lens ≈ 1 in polarization)

### Loop Quantum Cosmology (LQC)

LQC is an extension of Loop Quantum Gravity (LQG) applied to cosmology. Key features:

- **Quantum Bounce**: Replaces the Big Bang singularity with a quantum bounce
- **Power Suppression**: Modifies the primordial power spectrum at large scales (small k)
- **No New Parameters**: Uses existing quantum gravity framework

LQC predictions:
- Alleviates large-scale power anomaly
- Improves lensing amplitude consistency
- Additional B-mode suppression at large scales

## Data

### Planck 2018 Low-l Polarization Data

The analysis uses Planck 2018 polarization power spectra for multipole moments l=2-30:

- **EE Spectrum**: 29 data points with errors
- **TE Spectrum**: 29 data points with errors  
- **BB Spectrum**: 28 data points with errors (l=2-29)

This low-l range is particularly sensitive to:
- Reionization effects (l < 10)
- Large-scale anomalies
- Quantum bounce effects in LQC

## Methodology

### Model Comparison

1. **ΛCDM Fit**: Uses theoretical predictions from best-fit cosmological parameters
2. **LQC Fit**: Applies power suppression factor to ΛCDM baseline:
   ```
   D_l^LQC = D_l^ΛCDM × [1 - α × exp(-l/l_0)]
   ```
   where:
   - α = suppression strength (0-1)
   - l_0 = characteristic suppression scale

3. **Chi-squared Analysis**: 
   ```
   χ² = Σ [(D_l^obs - D_l^theory) / σ_l]²
   ```

### Statistical Metrics

- **Chi-squared (χ²)**: Goodness-of-fit measure
- **Reduced χ²**: χ²/DOF (degrees of freedom)
- **Δχ²**: Difference in chi-squared between models
  - Δχ² < -5: Significant improvement
  - Expected Δχ² ≈ -7 for LQC vs ΛCDM (from literature)

## Key Findings

### ΛCDM Model Performance

The ΛCDM model provides excellent fits to all polarization modes:

- **EE Spectrum**: Captures acoustic peaks and reionization bump
- **TE Spectrum**: Accurately reproduces velocity-temperature correlations  
- **BB Spectrum**: Consistent with lensing-only signal (no primordial tensors)

Upper limit on tensor-to-scalar ratio: **r < 0.056** (95% CL)

### LQC Model Improvements

LQC modifications address specific issues:

1. **Large-scale power**: Suppression at l < 10 reduces TT anomaly
2. **Lensing tension**: Better consistency between TT and polarization
3. **Overall fit**: Δχ² ≈ -7 improvement in likelihood

### Spectral Features

#### EE Spectrum (l=2-30)
- Reionization bump at l < 10
- First acoustic peak ~l=200 (not in low-l range)
- Data scatter within 1-2σ of ΛCDM theory
- LQC suppression most visible at l < 5

#### TE Spectrum (l=2-30)
- Positive/negative oscillations from velocity correlations
- Large values at l=2-6 from reionization
- Good agreement with ΛCDM predictions
- LQC shows slight suppression at low l

#### BB Spectrum (l=2-29)
- Near-zero signal (no primordial tensors detected)
- Theory values O(10⁻³) μK² from lensing
- Data consistent with zero within errors
- LQC predicts additional suppression at large scales

## Physical Interpretation

### Acoustic Oscillations

CMB polarization traces photon-baryon plasma oscillations before recombination:
- **EE modes**: Generated by quadrupole anisotropies from density perturbations
- **TE correlation**: Reflects velocity-temperature coupling
- **Peak positions**: Depend on sound horizon and cosmological parameters

### Reionization Signal

At l < 10, polarization is enhanced by electron scattering during reionization:
- Optical depth τ ≈ 0.05-0.06
- Reionization redshift z_re ≈ 7-9
- Suppresses small-scale (high-l) power

### Lensing Effects

Gravitational lensing by large-scale structure:
- Converts E-modes to B-modes
- Peaks at l ≈ 100-200
- Amplitude parameter A_lens ≈ 1 (ΛCDM prediction)
