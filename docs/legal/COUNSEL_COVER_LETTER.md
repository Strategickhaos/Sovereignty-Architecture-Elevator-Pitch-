## 1. What to Look at First for Patents

The following concepts represent the **core technical innovations** most likely to have patent-eligible subject matter. These should be your primary focus for IP protection strategy:

### A. TRIG6 Mathematical Framework
- **TRIG6 fitness function**: The specific combination of trigonometric phase (θ), resonance (R), drift (D), noise (N), and equivalence (eq) into a unified fitness metric
- **TRIG6 Danger Zone**: Use of tangent singularities (`|tan(θ)| > K`) as a universal danger detector across diverse domains
- **TRIG6 State Vector**: Normalized multi-domain state encoding for automated risk evaluation

**Why it matters:** This is a novel mathematical approach to process modeling, risk detection, and fitness evaluation that appears to be applicable across software, biomedical, materials science, and other domains.

**Action items:**
- Conduct prior art search on trigonometric process models and tangent-based danger detection
- Evaluate whether the specific combination and application domains constitute patentable subject matter
- Consider utility patent applications for the method and system claims

### B. FlameLang Compiler Architecture
- **Multi-stage compilation pipeline**: Natural language → semantic layer → TRIG6-encoded layer → codon IR → executable code
- **TRIG6-aware semantics**: Language design specifically integrated with TRIG6 evaluation at compile-time and runtime
- **Codon-based intermediate representation (IR)**: 64-entry instruction alphabet modeled on biological DNA codons

**Why it matters:** The coupling of a programming language with biological-inspired IR and TRIG6-based fitness evaluation represents a potentially novel compiler architecture.

**Action items:**
- Review prior art in domain-specific languages, genetic programming, and evolutionary compilation
- Assess whether the specific architecture constitutes patentable compilation methods
- Consider software copyright protection for the compiler implementation

### C. SAGCO-OS Evolutionary Runtime
- **TRIG6-integrated OS runtime**: Operating system that continuously evaluates code fitness using TRIG6 metrics
- **Autonomous code evolution**: OS-level self-healing and evolution based on fitness thresholds
- **Darwinian Gate mechanism**: Mutation, selection, and propagation of code variants at the OS level

**Why it matters:** Integration of evolutionary algorithms into OS runtime at this level may represent novel operating system design.

**Action items:**
- Search prior art in self-healing systems, autonomous computing, and evolutionary operating systems
- Evaluate whether OS-level integration of TRIG6 and codon-based evolution is patentable
- Consider system and method claims for the evolutionary runtime architecture

### D. Potentiometer Proof Engine
- **Hardware-software interface**: Physical analog input (potentiometer) mapped to TRIG6 parameters in real-time
- **Uncertainty quantification**: Use of analog input as controllable uncertainty/mutation source
- **Interactive evolution**: Human-in-the-loop evolutionary simulation via kinesthetic input

**Why it matters:** Novel apparatus and method for injecting real-world uncertainty into computational fitness models.

**Action items:**
- Evaluate whether this constitutes patentable apparatus and method
- Consider both hardware and software claims for the interface and mapping mechanism
- Document reduction to practice with working prototypes

### E. Recipe Gene Template System
- **TRIG6-encoded process representation**: Unified schema for representing heterogeneous processes (therapeutic, material, industrial) as TRIG6-compatible "genes"
- **Evolutionary process optimization**: Framework for simulating and evolving recipes under TRIG6 fitness constraints

**Why it matters:** Potential method claims for representing and optimizing complex processes across diverse domains.

**Action items:**

## 3. Where to Be Careful: Medical and Therapeutic Claims

**CRITICAL NOTICE:** Several components of the Strategickhaos ecosystem touch on medical, therapeutic, or health-related domains. These require **special scrutiny** to avoid:
- Unsubstantiated medical efficacy claims
- FDA regulatory issues
- Liability for health-related advice or products
- Patent eligibility issues under *Alice* and diagnostic method precedents

### Sensitive Components

#### A. NEURO-36 Genome
- **What it is:** Taxonomy of 36 neurological diseases modeled as TRIG6 process vectors
- **Risk:** Could be interpreted as diagnostic tool or medical device if presented as having clinical utility
- **Recommendation:** 
  - Frame explicitly as **research tool** and **theoretical modeling framework**
  - Avoid any claims of diagnostic accuracy or clinical validity without FDA clearance
  - Include prominent disclaimers that this is not medical advice or diagnostic tool
  - Consider whether to pursue patent claims at all, given *Alice* and diagnostic method uncertainties

#### B. Recipe Gene Templates (Therapeutic Applications)
- **What it is:** TRIG6 encoding of therapeutic recipes, including ancient and traditional remedies
- **Risk:** Could trigger FDA regulation if presented as drug development or therapeutic optimization tool
- **Recommendation:**
  - Frame as **historical preservation** and **computational modeling** of traditional knowledge
  - Do NOT claim therapeutic efficacy for any specific recipe without clinical trials and FDA approval
  - Include disclaimers: "Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease"
  - Consider trade secret protection for proprietary recipe databases rather than public patent disclosure

#### C. Therapeutic Basin Concept
- **What it is:** Region of TRIG6 state space representing "safe and effective" operating regimes
- **Risk:** Could be interpreted as medical claim if applied to therapeutic contexts
- **Recommendation:**
  - Define as **mathematical concept** applicable to any process optimization, not specifically medical
  - Use neutral examples (material curing, fermentation) alongside any therapeutic references
  - Avoid language suggesting clinical validation without supporting data

### General Guidance on Medical/Therapeutic Claims

**DO:**
- Frame all medical-adjacent concepts as research tools, theoretical models, or computational frameworks
- Include prominent disclaimers on all materials that touch medical domains
- Consult with FDA regulatory counsel if commercializing any health-related applications
- Document that any therapeutic applications are investigational and require clinical validation

**DON'T:**
- Claim diagnostic accuracy, therapeutic efficacy, or clinical utility without evidence
- Market any component as medical device or diagnostic tool without FDA clearance
- Use language that implies medical advice, treatment recommendations, or health outcomes
- File patent claims on diagnostic or therapeutic methods without regulatory strategy
