Medical research requires:
1. **Long-term stability** - Cures take decades, not quarters
2. **Data security** - Patient information must be unhackable
3. **Financial independence** - Research can't stop when funding dries up
4. **Audit trails** - Every dollar, every decision, fully traceable
5. **Attack resilience** - The work must continue despite adversaries

**The DOM OS provides all of this.**
