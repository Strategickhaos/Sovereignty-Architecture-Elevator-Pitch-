- **Vectorize diseases** using NEURO-36 + TRIG6

## The Path Forward: Open Questions

### Scientific Questions

1. **Do all diseases have TRIG6 vectors?**
   - Or only neurological conditions with wave-like dynamics?
   - Can we extend to cancer, autoimmune disorders, infections?

2. **Can we measure R, D, N clinically?**
   - What are the biomarkers?
   - How often must we sample?
   - Can patients self-report these metrics?

3. **Do fitness functions work for drug evolution?**
   - Can we simulate drug candidates in .t6?
   - Will Darwinian selection find treatments humans wouldn't?
   - How do we avoid overfitting to simulation vs. reality?

4. **What are the ethical implications?**
   - Who owns TRIG6 vectors of their diseases?
   - How do we prevent insurance discrimination based on D/N?
   - Can we open-source this without enabling harm?

### Technical Questions

5. **Can we scale .t6 simulations to molecular dynamics?**
   - Current sims are abstract (R, D, N parameters)
   - Need protein folding, receptor binding, metabolic pathways
   - Compute requirements are massive

6. **How do we validate TRIG6 medical predictions?**
   - Clinical trials are slow and expensive
   - Can we use retrospective data?
   - What accuracy threshold makes this useful?

7. **Can patients run their own simulations?**
   - Democratize access to TRIG6 tools?
   - Enable personalized medicine without experts?
   - Risks of misuse vs. benefits of empowerment?

### Strategic Questions

8. **How do we fund this research?**
   - Traditional pharma model (patents, exclusivity)?
   - Open source + mission-driven (Sister Protocol model)?
   - Hybrid (open research, commercial applications)?

9. **Who should lead this work?**
   - Academic researchers (slow, rigorous)?
   - Biotech startups (fast, risky)?
   - AI companies (compute-heavy, data-driven)?
   - Patient advocacy groups (mission-aligned)?

10. **What's the timeline to first cure?**
    - Optimistic: 3-5 years (epilepsy, migraine)
    - Realistic: 10-15 years (neurodegenerative)
    - Ambitious: 20+ years (complex multi-system diseases)
