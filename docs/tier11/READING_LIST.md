## 📊 READING STRATEGIES

### For Dense Papers

1. **First Pass** (10 min)
   - Read abstract
   - Skim introduction and conclusion
   - Look at figures
   - Decide if worth reading

2. **Second Pass** (1 hour)
   - Read carefully but skip proofs
   - Note key ideas
   - Note what you don't understand

3. **Third Pass** (multiple hours)
   - Work through proofs
   - Reproduce key results
   - Connect to other papers

### For Textbooks

1. **Survey** (30 min)
   - Read table of contents
   - Skim chapter summaries
   - Identify prerequisite knowledge

2. **Targeted Reading**
   - Focus on relevant chapters
   - Do every third exercise
   - Take detailed notes

3. **Deep Study**
   - Work every exercise
   - Prove every theorem yourself
   - Extend concepts

### For Videos

1. **Watch at 1.5x speed** first pass
2. **Take timestamped notes**
3. **Pause and think** at key points
4. **Watch again at 1x** if needed
5. **Implement concepts in code**
