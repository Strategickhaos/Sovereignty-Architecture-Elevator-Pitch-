# Swarm Onboarding Process

## Welcome to the Academy

You're not a student.
You're a contributor.

The moment you learn something?
You're qualified to teach it.

## Step 1: Read the Philosophy

Start with `/sovereign-engineering-academy/README.md`

Understand:
- Why this exists
- Why SMEs are credited
- How the multiplier works

## Step 2: Read DOM_FAILURES.md

Learn from the struggles.
Identify which match your current challenges.
Know that failure is part of the path.

## Step 3: Select Your Track

Choose based on:
- Current skill gaps
- Mission requirements
- Personal interests
- Time available

## Step 4: Access the SMEs

Each module lists the primary SME.
Ask questions.
Work through exercises.
Apply to real projects.

## Step 5: Document Your Journey

When you struggle: Document it.
When you break through: Document it.
When you apply it: Document it.

Others will follow your path.

## Step 6: Contribute Back

Found a better explanation? Add it.
Discovered a new application? Document it.
Created a useful tool? Share it.

The curriculum evolves through contribution.

## Welcome to the Loop

Learn. Apply. Teach. Improve. Repeat.
