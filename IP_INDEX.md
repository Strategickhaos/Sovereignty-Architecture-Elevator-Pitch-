## Disclosure Status Legend

- **Drafted**: Formal disclosure document written and hashed

- **Prototype**: Working implementation exists, IP strategy TBD

## Strategy Notes

### Defensive Publication
These inventions are published as **prior art** to prevent patenting by any party. They remain freely available for anyone to use, implement, or build upon.

### Trade Secret
Certain implementation details or business logic may be kept confidential while the core concepts are published as prior art.

### Potential Patent
In rare cases where defensive publication is not strategically optimal, patent application may be considered. This is the exception, not the rule.
