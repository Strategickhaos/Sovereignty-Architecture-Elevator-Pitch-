## Executive Summary

The **Strategickhaos Foundation** will be established as a separate 501(c)(3) tax-exempt charitable organization to receive the 7% irrevocable commitment from the Valoryield Engine. This structure provides:

- **Tax benefits** for the DAO LLC (charitable deduction)

## Strategic Rationale

### Why Separate 501(c)(3)?

**Instead of making the DAO itself a 501(c)(3), we create a receiving foundation:**

**DAO LLC Advantages (Maintained):**
- ✅ Flexible governance and voting
- ✅ Ability to generate profit and distribute to members
- ✅ Commercial partnerships and licensing
- ✅ Investment opportunities
- ✅ Less restrictive operations

**Foundation Advantages (Added):**
- ✅ Tax deductions for DAO contributions
- ✅ Grant funding eligibility
- ✅ Enhanced public trust
- ✅ IRS oversight provides legitimacy
- ✅ Perpetual existence (survives DAO dissolution)

**Result:** Best of both worlds — commercial flexibility + charitable legitimacy

## Part XI: Annual Compliance Requirements

### IRS Form 990 Filing

**Must file annually:**
- Form 990-N (e-postcard): if gross receipts < $50,000
- Form 990-EZ: if gross receipts < $200,000 and assets < $500,000
- Form 990: if above thresholds

**Our likely requirement:** Form 990-EZ initially, then Form 990

**Key 990 Sections:**
- Part I: Summary of revenue and expenses
- Part III: Program service accomplishments (narrative)
- Part VI: Governance and management
- Schedule B: Schedule of contributors (confidential to IRS)
- Schedule O: Supplemental information

**Due Date:** 5th month after fiscal year end (or 15th day with extension)

### State Compliance

**Annual requirements vary by state:**
- State charitable registration renewal
- State tax return (in some states)
- Annual report to Secretary of State
- Director/officer updates

### Audit Requirements

**Federal:**
- If expenditures > $750,000: Single Audit (OMB Uniform Guidance)
- If receiving federal grants: Compliance audit

**State:**
- Some states require annual audit if revenue exceeds threshold
- Check state-specific requirements

**Our Approach:**
- Voluntary annual financial review (Year 1-2)
- Independent audit starting Year 3 or when revenue > $500,000
- Use Antifragile Audit proofs to demonstrate controls
