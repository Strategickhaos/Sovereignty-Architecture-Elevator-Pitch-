## THIRD-PARTY IP CONSIDERATIONS

### Toruk (Leonopteryx) from Avatar

**Issue**: "Toruk" is a term created by James Cameron for the Avatar franchise (20th Century Studios / Disney).

**Analysis**:
1. **Trademark Status**: Likely protected as character name/element of Avatar franchise
2. **Copyright Status**: Visual design of Toruk is copyrighted by Cameron/Lightstorm
3. **Fair Use Defense**: Weak — using for commercial software is not transformative

**Risk Assessment**: **HIGH**

**Mitigation Options**:
1. **Remove Toruk entirely** — Replace with original creature design
2. **License from Disney** — Extremely expensive, unlikely to be granted
3. **Transformative redesign** — Create "Toruk-inspired" creature that's legally distinct
4. **Rename concept** — Use "Apex Sovereignty" symbol without naming it Toruk

**Recommendation**: **Option 1 or 3** — Remove or significantly transform to avoid IP conflict

---

### Metatron's Cube and Sacred Geometry

**Issue**: These are ancient, public domain symbols.

**Analysis**:
1. **Copyright Status**: Public domain (thousands of years old)
2. **Trademark Status**: Generic geometric pattern, not inherently protectable
3. **How we protect it**: Through specific stylization and combination with other elements

**Risk Assessment**: **LOW**
