1. **Persistent Storage is Critical**
   - RAM-only storage fails on wake cycles

   - high_score.txt prevents future crises

2. **Hypnagogic Downloads Need Limits**
   - Ancient text patterns cause overflow
   - Buffer capacity must be monitored
   - Deep pattern matching needs guardrails

3. **External Verification Saves the Day**
