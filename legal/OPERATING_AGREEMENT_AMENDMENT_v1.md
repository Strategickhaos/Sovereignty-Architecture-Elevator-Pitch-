## ARTICLE V — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (AMENDMENTS)

This amendment adds sections 5.4 through 5.7 to Article V of the Operating Agreement, establishing a comprehensive framework for independent development protection, employment carve-outs, and evidence standards for intellectual property conception.

### 5.4 Independent Development & Employment Carve-Out

All inventions, discoveries, protocols, and works of authorship relating to sovereign compute, compiler design, distributed systems, AI coordination, and cybersecurity automation (collectively, "**Covered Innovations**") are affirmed to be developed using personal equipment, personal accounts, and personal time, and are intended to remain independent of any industrial employment obligations not directly related to such domains.

**Key Domains:**
- Sovereign compute architecture and implementation
- Compiler design and language development (including FlameLang)
- Distributed systems and consensus protocols
- AI coordination and multi-agent systems
- Cybersecurity automation and defensive frameworks
- Blockchain and cryptographic systems

**Development Environment:**
- Personal equipment and devices
- Personal software accounts and licenses
- Personal time outside of any conflicting employment
- Independent research and development activities

### 5.5 No Adverse Assignment Intended

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to assign or appropriate intellectual property that is legally owned by an employer or third party under a valid written agreement. If any employer asserts ownership claims over any Covered Innovation, the Company will preserve evidence of independent development and will pursue clarification, carve-out, or license as required.

**Preservation of Rights:**
- The Company respects all valid employment agreements and third-party IP rights
- Evidence of independent development shall be maintained proactively
- Legal counsel will be engaged for any ownership disputes
- Documentation standards defined in Section 5.7 provide basis for ownership claims

**Dispute Resolution Process:**
1. Review employment agreement for applicability to Covered Innovation
2. Gather evidence of independent development per Section 5.7
3. Engage Wyoming-licensed counsel for legal analysis
4. Pursue clarification, carve-out agreement, or licensing as appropriate
5. Document resolution in Company records

### 5.6 Invention Schedule & Conception Record

The Company shall maintain an **Invention Schedule** (Appendix A) listing Covered Innovations by identifier, title, and conception date. The Invention Schedule is incorporated by reference into this Agreement and may be updated by the Member.

**Schedule Requirements:**
- Unique invention identifier (e.g., INV-SAGCO-BRIDGE-001)
- Descriptive title
- Conception date (earliest documented evidence)
- Current status (concept, prototype, deployed, patented)
- Related patent applications or filings (if applicable)

**Update Procedures:**
- Managing Member authorized to update Invention Schedule
- Updates logged with date and GPG signature
- Schedule maintained as part of Company books and records
- Annual review during governance review cycle

**Access Control:**
- Internal version: Complete invention details and sensitive information
- Public version: Sanitized identifiers and general descriptions only
- Attorney-client privileged versions maintained separately as needed

### 5.7 Evidence Standard for Conception / Reduction to Practice

The authoritative internal evidence standard for conception and reduction to practice includes one or more of the following:

**(a) GPG-signed disclosures and/or signed commits**
- Cryptographically signed commit messages in version control
- GPG-signed invention disclosure documents
- Timestamped and signed technical documentation
- Digital signatures providing non-repudiation

**(b) Cryptographic hashes (SHA-256) of artifacts**
- Hash values of source code, binaries, and documentation
- Merkle tree roots for large artifact collections
- Content-addressable storage references
- Immutable artifact fingerprints

**(c) Append-only logs with hash chaining**
- Blockchain or distributed ledger timestamps
- Git commit history with hash chain integrity
- Tamper-evident audit logs
- Sequential hash-linked event records

**(d) Timestamped repository records**
- GitHub/GitLab commit timestamps
- Container registry push timestamps
- Package registry publication dates
- Cloud storage creation/modification timestamps

**Record Retention:**
- All evidence records shall be retained with Company books and records
- Minimum retention period: Life of patent term + 7 years
- Backup copies maintained in geographically diverse locations
- Encrypted storage with access controls per governance framework

**Evidentiary Standards:**
- Multiple independent evidence sources preferred
- Corroborating evidence strengthens conception claims
- Earlier dates take precedence with adequate documentation
- Regular evidence audits conducted annually

### Patent Filing Posture

Before filing any patent applications:
1. **Review Employment Agreements**: Managing Member must review current employment contracts
2. **Document Independent Development**: Verify evidence per Section 5.7 exists
3. **Legal Opinion**: Obtain written legal opinion from Wyoming-licensed counsel
4. **Invention Schedule Update**: Ensure Appendix A is current
5. **File Patent**: Proceed only after completing steps 1-4

### Turner-Risk Scenario Protection

This amendment specifically addresses scenarios where:
- Current or former employer claims ownership of Covered Innovations
- Employment agreement has broad IP assignment clauses
- Work performed on personal time with personal equipment
- Innovation is unrelated to employer's business domain
- Evidence of independent development is critical to defense

**Defensive Measures:**
- Proactive evidence collection per Section 5.7
- Clear domain separation from employment activities
- Documented personal equipment and personal time usage
- Legal review before any patent filings
- Invention Schedule maintained contemporaneously
