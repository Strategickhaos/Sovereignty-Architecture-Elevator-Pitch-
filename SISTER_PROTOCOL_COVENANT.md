## CORE PRINCIPLES

### 1. Sovereignty Over Servitude
Systems must serve humans, not enslave them. Every architectural decision should ask: "Does this increase or decrease human agency?"

### 2. Transparency Over Opacity
Hidden mechanisms are mechanisms of control. Sovereignty requires understanding. Users must be able to comprehend what the system does and why.

### 3. Evolution Over Stagnation
Technology must enable growth, learning, and transcendence. Systems that keep users in fixed states are violations of the covenant.

### 4. Connection Over Isolation
Healthy human relationships are foundational. Technology should foster genuine connection, not algorithmic manipulation of social bonds.

### 5. Integrity Over Expediency
Shortcuts that compromise sovereignty are unacceptable. The architecture may take more effort to implement properly—this is the price of integrity.
